# Asset provenance

## assets/skyline.png

Original panorama generated for this project with the built-in Imagegen tool on October 2, 2026. Saved locally and consumed by `src/renderer.js`. The four user-supplied reference images informed the requested mood; they are not bundled or redistributed.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: panoramic parallax background for an original side-on 2D cyberpunk platform game called NULL RAIN. Create an exquisite atmospheric ultrawide 3:1 environment painting, 3072x1024 if possible. A dense immense rainy nocturnal Asian-inspired vertical megacity: layered dark teal industrial towers, immense cyan holographic light panels, hot magenta vertical signs with abstract geometric glyphs, warm amber inhabited windows, covered elevated walkways suspended between towers, ducts, satellite arrays, exposed wiring, soft distant fog, lush detailed architecture. Hand-painted pixel-art inspired texture but cinematic restrained palette and clear silhouettes; high-end independent 2D game background. Orthographic straight side-on elevation, horizontal level composition, no vanishing-point road, no street perspective. Tall architecture fills entire frame with atmospheric depth, dark indigo sky at very top, far buildings dissolve in blue rain at base. Readable rich saturated neon accents against midnight architecture. Background layer only: no people, player characters, foreground platforms, HUD, title, logos, watermarks or readable text. Left and right edges dark low-contrast building masses so it tiles unobtrusively. Distinct variation across skyline and intriguing giant circular antenna structures. The image should have strong atmospheric depth and many authored small details, not generic repetitive rectangles.

## Procedural visuals and audio

Characters, weapons, platforms, architecture, weather, effects, signs, HUD and menus are authored in this project's source code. Original music and SFX are synthesized by `src/audio.js`; no external audio files or licenses are required.
